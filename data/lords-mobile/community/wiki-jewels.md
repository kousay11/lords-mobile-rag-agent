- Jewels** are items that can be inlaid into Equipment to provide various boosts.

1. Facts about jewels

- All currently-owned jewels can be viewed in the Cabinet.
- 4 jewels of the same type can be fused together to form a higher grade jewel with more potent boosts.
- Each piece of equipment can have a maximum of 3 jewels inlaid at a time. However, you cannot inlay two identical jewels into the same item.
-   Removing a jewel will destroy it, unless a Chisel of the same grade or higher is used.

1. How to Obtain

Most standard jewels can be obtained through Admin Quests and Guild Quests, or through Jewel Chests, which can be purchased with either Gems or Guild Coins. Jewel chests can also be obtained in events or purchased in special bundles.

Monster jewels can be found in their respective chests. Bundle jewels, turf jewels, and assault jewels can only be purchased in the Mall. Royal jewels and Feudal jewels can only be obtained in their respective events.

The Hoarder familiar's Elder Skill, when maxed, grants 3 random standard jewels (Common to Legendary) every 3 days.
